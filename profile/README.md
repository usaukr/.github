## What we are

A reviewed directory — not a scrape. UsaUkr helps people find Ukrainian-connected places and services near them, and helps those places be found.

## What you'll find

- 🏪 **Businesses & services** — from everyday needs to professionals who speak your language
- 📅 **Events** — a calendar that is reviewed before it is published
- 🏛 **Community organizations** — groups that keep the community together
- 🗺 **By city** — explore Ukrainian communities one city at a time

## Trust that means something

Every listing says what has actually been checked. If we haven't verified something, we don't pretend to — a trust signal is only useful if it is honest.

## Take part

Know a place we are missing? **[Tell us on usaukr.com](https://usaukr.com)** — suggestions go through the same review as everything else.
